# Metrics

* [Loan Delinquency Rate](./loan_delinquency_rate.md) - Percentage of active loans with overdue payments
* [NPA Ratio](./npa_ratio.md) - Non-performing asset ratio against total loan book
* [Transaction Success Rate](./transaction_success_rate.md) - Percentage of transactions completed successfully
* [KYC Completion Rate](./kyc_completion_rate.md) - Percentage of customers with verified KYC status
